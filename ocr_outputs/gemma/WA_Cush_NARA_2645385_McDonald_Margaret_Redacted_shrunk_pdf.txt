**Note at top:** answered 8/26
**Location/Date:** Nespelem Wash, Aug 16, 1918
**Recipient:** Mr Hammond

**Body:**
I am dropping you a few lines to let you know I am well and hope you folks are all well over there. Well I am going to tell you that there is a school here and I want to go to school here and if you dont care I will stay here and wont go back to Cushman for it is too far to go. and if you say it is all right I will stay at home and go to school write and let me know if you please Please write soon and let me know

### Page 6: Typewritten Letter
**Header:** Cushman Indian School, Tacoma, Washington.
**Date:** April 20, 1917.
**Recipient:** J. M. Johnson, Supt., Colville Indian Agency.

**Dear Sir:**
I have a letter from Mr. Arthur Hayward of Oroville, Washington, stating that he wishes his neice Margaret McDonald to remain here during the summer and reenter school in the fall. Her cousin Dora Noyes is also here and has agreed to stay through the summer provided she can get some spending money.

Both of these girls say that you have some money belonging to them and would like very much to have you send them some so that they will have some spending money during the vacation period. If they have any money under your supervision, I would like very much for you to send them a small amount for spending money during the summer as I think it will make them more contented.

**Very respectfully,**
[Signature]
Superintendent.
**Reference:** TBW-C
