May 8-12

Supt Friedman:-
Dear Sir -
I in reply to yours last letter which I received some time ago, I was indeed glad to hear from you, I am sorry that I couldn't answer sooner because I was so busy at the time I received your letter I have 60 Acres in alfalfa hay and it keep me busy irrigating it every day, I tell you when a man has to depend upon water for living that means he has do some hard digging.

***

**Page 9**

In the spring time the farmers here are very busy making ditches all over their fields so as to carry the water what is needed to irrigate these crops, another thing we do is that we cut our alfalfa hay three times in one season.
My wife and I has a new comer in our family it is the cutest little baby girl and we are both proud of it
I do wish to have the Red-Man mailed also to me They have change my mail box number now instead of #2'2 it is now #4'4.
I must close your letter hoping to hear from you.
Yours Truly
Frank Andrews

**Page 10**

Frank Andrews, an ex-student, now living at Wapato, Washington, writes: "It has been a long time since I left the school, but the Arrow is still a great help to me. I feel lost when I don't hear the news from Carlisle. I am well and happy and hope my friends at school are the same. I send my best regards to all of them."
