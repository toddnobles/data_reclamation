UNITED STATES INDIAN SCHOOL, CARLISLE, PENNSYLVANIA.

Name: Daniel Varner
1. Are you married and if so to whom? Yes to Miss Hettie Guard
2. What is your present address? Tacoma Wash Station P.
3. Did you attend or graduate from any other schools after leaving Carlisle? Give names of school and dates if possible. none
4. What is your present occupation? Salary? Carpenter, for rough carpentering .45¢ an hour, and finishing I get .50¢ an hour
5. Do you own your home? Yes
6. What kind of a house is it? Number of rooms? It is a wooden house, cedar siding shingle roof painted, gutters with iron spouts four rooms, with pantry and closet

Stock [blank]

**Page 4**

12. Tell me anything else of interest connected with your life. I have held these public offices since leaving Carlisle. first was Justice of the Peace for four years Road Supervisor for three years Dep. County Assessor for eight successive years. These offices I held under the Republican Election of the County I live in. And at present I am Depo game warden of which I was appointed by the Chief Warden.
