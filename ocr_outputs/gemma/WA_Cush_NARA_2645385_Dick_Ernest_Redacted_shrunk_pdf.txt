APPLICATION FOR ENROLLMENT IN A NONRESERVATION SCHOOL.
(For a child not enrolled at an Agency.)
For and in consideration of the United States assuming the care, education, and maintenance in the United States Indian School at Cushman Indian School, of Ernest Dick male, 16, I, Dixie Wanam of Puyallup P. O., State of Wash. do hereby voluntarily consent and agree to his enrollment in said school for a period of three years, and also obligate and bind myself to abide by all the rules and regulations for Indian schools.
I further say that the said child was born at Puyallup, Wash. on Oct. 31-1903 that the father, Marcus Dick was Indian of the Puyallup Tribe located at Cushman Agency; that he left the tribe about Dead 8 yrs that the mother, Daisy Dick was Indian of the Puyallup Tribe located at Cushman Agency, and left the tribe about [blank]; that the said child was born and reared in the United States, and now actually resides therein; and that he has attended the following schools:

Ernest Dick was sent in broken
out just like small pox. But he
had it when the rest did. I have
put him up stairs in the rear.
Called Dr. but he was out on a

Tell the folks at Office to come see

Very truly
Mrs. Culbertson

**Page 12**
[Handwritten letter]
My dear Mr. Hammond, after a visit
to Ernest Dick. The Doctor asked me
to say he had Tuberculosis Broncho Pneumonia
His Grand Father & mother were to see him Yesterday
Dr. Kinneras says the boy wants to go home.
and when he becomes a little stronger he
thinks it would possibly be the best
thing to let him go, as it might help
him to have the change. He did not say
so but, I do not believe he thinks he
will hold out so very long as he
has had it before.

Very truly
Mrs. S. C. Culbertson.
5-19-1919
