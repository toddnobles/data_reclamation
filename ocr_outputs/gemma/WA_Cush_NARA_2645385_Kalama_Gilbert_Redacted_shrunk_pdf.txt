Nisqually, Wash.
May 28-1920

Mr. Peele.
Dear Sir.

My Brother Gilbert Kalama left his Slide Trombone there at Cushman, and the cloths that he's entitled to, but he was so excited the day we were after him to go home, that he didn't take all they but his shoes with him. He said he was suppose to have his uniform and he left after things there in his room. He wants me to get these things, if they haven't been taken especially his Slide as his papa gave him the Slide Trombone. Please let me know whether his things are there and that we can get them.

Very Respectfully,
Mrs. Esther Hicks

Mrs. Esther Hicks,

Dear Madam:

Replying to your letter of may 28th, Gilbert's slide trombone is here. You can get it when you wish. If mr. Freeman has not already packed the uniform clothing for shipment, a uniform can be issued to Gilbert.

Yours respectfully,
Clerk in Charge.

Warm Springs or, Oregon
June 17-1919.

Supt. Hammond
Cushman Inst.
Tacoma, Wash.

Sir-

Please send Irene and Gilbert home, as soon as school is out, do not let them wait. I wish saw that they will return to school, in Sept. Sorry I have not the money to deposit but, know I will have money so can send them back to school. Thanking you for your care over them. I am your.

Respectfully-
Lillie Kuckup

Cushman Indian School,
Tacoma, Wash, Jan. 10, 1919.

Mrs. Nicholson,
Mr. T. J. Thompson;

Mrs. Lillie Kuckup, mother to Irene and Gilbert Kalama, claims sole custody by law of these two children, and objects to their visiting Mr. Peter Kalama, or being placed in his custody even temporarily.

Inasmuch as these children live with their mother and were placed in school by her, we must comply with her request.

Very respectfully,
B. H. Hammond
Superintendent

**Page 9**

Cushman Indian School,
Tacoma, Wash, Oct. 25, 1918.

Mrs. Naomi Wagner,
Warmsprings, Oregon.

Dear Mrs. Wagner:

In reply to your letter of the 23rd instant, would advise that your brother Gilbert Kalama was ill with influenza, but has fully recovered and is as well as ever.

Irene and Winona are both very well indeed and I hope they will not fall ill.

I am sorry Meta Tewee has no funds with which to pay her transportation to Cushman. She is a desirable pupil, but we cannot pay her transportation back to school a second time in three years. Please do your best to obtain her return at her own expense as promised when she left for home last June.

Yours very truly,
Superintendent

BHH-S
Reproduced at the National Archives at Seattle
