Cushman Indian School
Tacoma, Wash., Nov. 6, 1919

Mr. Don M. Carr, Supt.
Yakima Indian Agency
Ft. Simcoe, Wash.

Dear Sir:

I am forwarding the application form for William Doyle, age 17, for your signature and for the signature of Mrs. Philisiet Cook, guardian, of White Swan.

Kindly have Mrs. Cook sign the application and return it to this office as soon as practicable.

Thanking you in advance, I am

Very respectfully,

Superintendent.

/MG
Enc.

Cushman Indian School
Tacoma, Wash., July 8, 1919

Mr. William Doyle
Moxie City, Wash.

Dear William:

Teddy Caisse found your Cushman Arrowhead and turned it in to Mr. Barton by request. I am forwarding it to you by this mail recommending greater care in safeguarding it.

Please tell Miss Doris not to forget that she is on the list of girls to demonstrate in Domestic Science at the Cushman booth during the Yakima fair.

Wishing you a very pleasant vacation and an early return to school, I am

Sincerely yours,

Superintendent.

EHH:MG
