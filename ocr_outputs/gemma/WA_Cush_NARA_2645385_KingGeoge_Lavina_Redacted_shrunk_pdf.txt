**CERTIFICATE OF PHYSICIAN.**
I HEREBY CERTIFY that on **Oct. 20 - 1919** I made a careful examination of the physical condition of **Lavinia King George**, the child named in the foregoing application, and found **her** to be **in proper physical condition to attend school**.

I recommend that the said child be enrolled in this school.
This **20th** day of **Oct.**, 1919.
**Claude W. Kinman, M.D.**
*School Physician.*

**The law provides:**
"That hereafter no appropriation, except appropriations made pursuant to treaties, shall be used to educate children of less than one-fourth Indian blood whose parents are citizens of the United States and of the State wherein they live and where there are adequate free school facilities provided." (Act of May 25, 1918 (40 Stat. L., 564).)

**5-192**
**APPLICATION FOR ENROLLMENT IN A NONRESERVATION SCHOOL.**
*(For a child not enrolled at an Agency.)*
For and in consideration of the United States assuming the care, education, and maintenance in the United States Indian School at **Jacoma, Wash.**, of **Lavinia King George, F, 11**, I, **Henry King George** of **Auburn, Wash.**, do hereby voluntarily consent and agree to **her** enrollment in said school for a period of **three** years...

I further say that the said child was born at **Auburn** on **? 1906**;
that the father, **Henry King George** is a Indian of the **Muckleshoot** Tribe located at **Cushman** Agency...
that the mother, **Mary King George** is a Indian of the **Muckleshoot** Tribe located at **Cushman** Agency...

**School History:**
*   **St Georges**, Jacoma Wash, 1915 - 1916, Grade: First
*   **Cushman**, Jacoma Wash, 1916, Grade: First

I further agree that said pupil shall be returned to the **Cushman** school on or before the last day of the vacation leave of absence...
This **9** day of **January**, 1918
**Two witnesses:** Lucy N. Smith, Oscar Hellen
**Henry King George** (Parent, guardian, or next of kin.)
P. O., **Auburn, Wash.**

**VOUCHER OF PERSON ARRANGING FOR TRANSFER.**
