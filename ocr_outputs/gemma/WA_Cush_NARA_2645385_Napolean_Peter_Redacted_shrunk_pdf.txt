### Page 3: Letter

Mr. E. H. Hammond, Supt. Wapata, Wash.
Cushman Agency
**FOR FILE**
CUSHMAN INDIAN SCHOOL
TACOMA, WASHINGTON
JUN 13 1918

Dear Sir - I take great pleasure to write you, in regards to my children whom you have in your charge. I would be most gratified if you can let them come for the vacation. They were here last vacation and I have a better position for Edna this vacation to work for the same folks I am working for at present at $10.00 a week as a helper around the house. They are white folks and are very good and appreciative. They have 100 acres of alfalfa hay to cut 3 times this season which I have been irrigating and will start to cut about next monday. Also have 60 acres in barley and will constantly need help all summer. My children's names are Edna & Peter Napoleon respectively. Peter will also be given something to do to keep him in good sort. I have a little niece there also named Sadie Napoleon, Mrs. Dan Secena of the Chehalis Reservation would like to take her for this vacation. They are also very good folks. I would desire you to put them on the train at Tacoma in the morning about 7 a.m. to Wapata. it would be convenient for us to meet on Saturday afternoon so that would give Edna time to enter upon her duties the following Monday. Please let me know when you could get them away. I am sending you a check for their fares. I will await your answer with much anxiety. Remaining yours Truly,
Al Napoleon
R.F.D. #1, Wapata, Wash.

P.S. Since I have started to work here I have made good, $60 first month $65.00 second month $85 for this 3rd month, and the children can do the same if they will earnestly do their work. Help is scarce in this locality this season owing to the work.
