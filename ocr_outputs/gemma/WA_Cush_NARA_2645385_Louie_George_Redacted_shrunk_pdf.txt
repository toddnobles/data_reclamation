**Page 4**

(Handwritten: Jule)

Cushman Indian School,
Tacoma, Wash, Dec, 16, 1918.

Supt. Fred C. Morgan,
Colville Agency,
Nespelem, Wash.

Dear Mr. Morgan;

I have your letter of December 10th, Please advise Mrs. Chuchuwalx, the grandmother of George Louie that her grandson is well and getting along nicely in school. The Flu left Cushman long ago and there is nothing to worry about.

Harry Sorimpt is still in the hospital recovering from the attack of influenza and pneumonia. He is in no immediate danger, is only kept at the hospital to grow strong, and to protect him form exposure and a relapse.

Lattie Friedlander is still in the hospital, but improving every day.

Nancy Moses has been in the hospital a few days with a headache; and Annie Bourgeau has the rheumatism; all the other Colville children are well. If any inquiries are made by parents, please advise them.

Very respectfully,

SBH-S
Superintendent
