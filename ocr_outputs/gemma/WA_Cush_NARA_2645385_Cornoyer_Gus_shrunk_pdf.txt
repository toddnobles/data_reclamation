DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
UNITED STATES INDIAN SERVICE
Umatilla Indian School,
Pendleton, Oreg., April 22, 1920.

[Stamp: CUSHMAN INDIAN SCHOOL RECEIVED APR 26 1920 TACOMA, WASHINGTON]

Mr. C. V. Peel,
Special Agent in Charge,
Cushman Indian School,
Tacoma, Washington.

Dear Mr. Peel:
Gus Cornoyer, deserter from your school, has arrived here on the reservation. I do not believe it advisable to return him for the short remainder of the term, as I understand he paid his own expense to the school. However, I will be guided by your wishes in the matter if you will advise me.

Very truly yours,
[Signature] E. L. Swartzlander, Superintendent.

[Handwritten note]: Tell Mr. P. that I concur in his belief, etc, & thank him for his co-operation. CVP.

Cushman Indian School
Tacoma, Wash., April 2, 1920

E. L. Swartzlander, Supt.
Umatilla Indian Agency
Pendleton, Oregon

Dear Sir:
Gus Cornoyer, a pupil from your Agency, received a letter from home telling him that his stock was running loose and things going to pieces, and that it would be to his interest to return and look after his affairs.
Kindly investigate and let me have an early reply.

Very respectfully,
Special Agent in Charge.

5-1142
DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR
UNITED STATES INDIAN SERVICE
Umatilla Indian School,
Pendleton, Oreg., April 5, 1920.

[Stamp: CUSHMAN INDIAN SCHOOL RECEIVED APR 8 1920 TACOMA, WASHINGTON]

Mr. C. V. Peel,
Special Agent in Charge,
Cushman Indian School,
Tacoma, Washington.

Dear Mr. Peel:
I have received your letter of the 2nd inst., concerning Gus Cornoyer. Gus' people do not live here, and when he was here he stayed with Charley Van Pelt and his family. Charley died last winter, and his widow has had some trouble with her stock getting away from her, but I do not know whether Gus had any stock with hers or whether he had not.
So far as I know there is no pressing need for Gus to return to the reservation.

Very truly yours,
[Signature] E. L. Swartzlander, Superintendent.

Reproduced at the National Archives at Seattle

(COPY of Supt. Swartzlander's letter)

Umatilla Indian School
Pendleton,
Oregon, Jan. 5, 1920

Supt. E. H. Hammond
Cushman Indian School
Tacoma, Washington

My dear Mr. Hammond:
The bearer is David Minthorn of this reservation, who with Joseph Wild Bill or Thompson, and possibly Gus Cornoyer, wish to enter your school for the balance of this term. They will pay their own transportation, and I send you this letter to let you know who they are, and that they go with my endorsement. I hope you can find room for them. I would appreciate it if you will let me know when they arrive at Cushman.

Yours sincerely,
E. L. SWARTZLANDER
Superintendent.
