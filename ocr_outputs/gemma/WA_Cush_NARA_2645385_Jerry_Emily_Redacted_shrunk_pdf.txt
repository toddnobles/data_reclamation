5-192a
APPLICATION FOR ENROLLMENT IN A NONRESERVATION SCHOOL
(For a child enrolled at an Agency.)

... United States Indian School at CUSHMAN AGENCY TACOMA, WASHINGTON
of Emily Jerry female; age 9; date of birth 1908;

NAME OF FATHER: Chas Jerry, Chas Dominich. TRIBE: Muckleshoot.
NAME OF MOTHER: Mary Jerry, Lothe Dominich. TRIBE: Muckleshoot.

I, Jerry Dominich, do hereby voluntarily consent and agree to her enrollment in said school for a period of 3 years...
Signature: Jerry Dominich (Parent, guardian, or next of kin)
P.O. address: Auburn, Wash.
Witness: Chas. A Reynolds.
Note: Enrolled sep. 18-1917

CERTIFICATE OF AGENT OR BONDED SUPERINTENDENT.
"...Lives 1 1/2 miles from public school. Cushman Indian - parent wants govt - unable to pay tuition. Says children need treatment for kidney trouble."
This 18 day of Sept, 1918.

CERTIFICATE OF SCHOOL PHYSICIAN.
I hereby certify that on May 30th 1919 I made a careful examination of the physical condition of Emily Jerry... and found her to be in proper physical condition to attend school.
This 26th day of May, 1919. Signed C. Claud H. Kinner, M.D.
Reexam: Oct 1st 1919 S.E.C.
